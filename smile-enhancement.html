Smile enhancement options

Different details, considered together.

Here are some of the treatments you can ask about. A dentist can explain what each involves, its limits and whether it may suit your individual needs.

Veneers

Thin coverings bonded to the front of selected teeth may be discussed to change their appearance. Assessment includes tooth condition, bite, alternatives and long-term care.

Zirconia crowns

A crown covers a tooth and may be considered when a tooth needs more extensive restoration. The dentist can discuss material choices, preparation and maintenance.

Teeth whitening

Whitening may help change the shade of natural teeth. A dental check can identify causes of discolouration and explain what results and sensitivity to expect.

Digital smile designing

Digital photographs or scans may support a discussion about possible smile changes. A preview is a planning aid, not a guarantee of the final result.

Gum contouring

For some people, reshaping the gum line may be discussed after checking gum health, tooth proportions and the cause of the concern.

A considered approach

Start with your goals and oral health.

Cosmetic and restorative options can involve different trade-offs. Your dentist can help you compare suitable choices and take time to answer your questions.

01

Talk through what matters to you

Share the changes you’re considering and any questions or concerns.

02

Have your teeth and gums assessed

The clinician checks oral health and discusses any records that may help.

03

Compare appropriate options

Review likely steps, alternatives, upkeep, limitations and costs before deciding.

04

Choose your next step

If you choose to proceed, your care team will explain the plan and follow-up.
