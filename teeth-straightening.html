Teeth straightening in Thrissur

Braces and aligners for a straighter smile.

Orthodontic treatment can use fixed braces or removable clear aligners to guide teeth over time. The right approach depends on your teeth, bite, oral health and goals. An orthodontist can explain the options after an assessment.

Two common approaches

Braces or clear aligners?

Both are designed to move teeth gradually. The best fit depends on clinical findings and what you can comfortably maintain day to day.

⌁

Braces

Small brackets are attached to the teeth and connected with a wire. Your orthodontist adjusts the appliance during follow-up visits as your treatment progresses.

  • Fixed in place during treatment
  • Different brace materials may be discussed
  • Needs careful brushing around brackets and wires
◇

Clear aligners

A series of custom-made clear trays fits over the teeth. Aligners are removable for meals and cleaning, and need to be worn as directed for treatment to progress.

  • Removable for eating and cleaning
  • Needs consistent wear and care
  • Suitability depends on your individual case

Not sure which sounds right? That’s okay. At a consultation, the Legacy Dental Hub team can assess your needs and discuss whether braces, aligners or another option may be appropriate.

What to expect

Your orthodontic treatment journey

Visits and timing vary from person to person. Your orthodontist will talk you through the plan and what follow-up it involves.

01

Assessment

Discuss what you’d like to change. The orthodontist examines your teeth and bite and may recommend records such as photographs, X-rays or scans.

02

Personalised plan

Talk through suitable options, expected visits, care at home, likely costs and questions before deciding how to proceed.

03

Progress reviews

Attend follow-up appointments so your orthodontist can review progress and make any needed adjustments to your plan.

04

Retention

After active treatment, a retainer may be recommended to help maintain the result. Follow your orthodontist’s guidance.

Good oral care matters. Braces can make cleaning more challenging, and removable aligners need regular cleaning too. Keep up with your dental check-ups and ask your care team how to look after your teeth and appliance.
