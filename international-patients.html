International patient enquiries

Planning dental care in Thrissur?

If you are considering dental care during a visit to Kerala, contact Legacy Dental Hub before making travel plans. The clinic can confirm whether it can accommodate your enquiry and the dates you have in mind. A dentist needs to assess your individual needs before recommending treatment.

A clear first step

Plan your enquiry before you travel

Share a little about your planned visit and what you would like to discuss. The clinic can then let you know whether an appointment may be possible.

01

Send an enquiry

Tell the clinic which dates you expect to be in Thrissur, how you prefer to be contacted, and the kind of dental concern or treatment you want to discuss.

02

Confirm availability

Ask whether the clinic can see you during your visit and what information to bring. Please wait for confirmation before arranging travel around an appointment.

03

Discuss a care plan

At a consultation, the dentist can assess your needs and discuss suitable options, expected appointments, follow-up, alternatives and estimated costs.

Allow time for an in-person assessment

Online messages can help start a conversation, but they cannot confirm a diagnosis or treatment plan. Timing and visit requirements vary with each person’s clinical assessment.

Topics to discuss with the dentist

Questions about implants or veneers?

These notes can help you prepare questions. They do not confirm that a treatment is offered at the clinic or that it is right for you.

Treatment enquiry

Dental implants

Ask whether implants may be an option for your situation, what examination or imaging the dentist may need, what alternatives are available, and how appointments, healing, restoration and follow-up may affect your visit schedule. Timing depends on individual assessment.

Treatment enquiry

Dental veneers

Ask whether veneers are available and appropriate for your goals, what alternatives may suit, whether tooth preparation is involved, and what maintenance, limitations, appointments and costs to consider.

ⓘAbout dental records: Please contact the clinic before sending scans, photographs, medical history or other dental records. Ask what method is appropriate for sharing them securely. Avoid sending sensitive records through ordinary email or WhatsApp unless the clinic confirms an approved process.
