A few helpful answers

Good to know.

Have another question? We’re happy to help you find the information you need.

How do I book my first appointment?

Contact Legacy Dental Hub using the clinic’s phone number or contact details below. The team can help find a suitable time and let you know what to bring.

What should I expect at my first visit?

Your dentist will ask about your dental history and what you’d like help with, then explain any recommended next steps. You can ask questions at any point.

Can I ask about treatment options before deciding?

Of course. The team can talk you through options, answer your questions and help you understand the next steps before you make a decision.

What should I do if I have an urgent concern?

Contact the clinic as soon as possible and explain what is happening. The team can advise you on the appropriate next step.
